Abstract:
To solve the feature extraction problem of one-dimensional heart sound time series, this paper proposes a spatio-temporal matrix imaging method combined with dual-stream convolution network (SMI-DSNet). I independently completed dataset construction, ablation comparison experiments, paper writing and revision. The lightweight time-series modeling framework can be applied to multi-modal encoders, lightweight time-series large models and audio representation learning.
